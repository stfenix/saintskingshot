Game Guide

Quick reference for key game systems, milestones, and mechanics. Based on official Kingshot help center data.

⚔️ Chief Gear
Chief Gear — Unlocks at TC Lv 22
Chief Charm — Unlocks at TC Lv 25
⚠️ Both are PERMANENT once equipped — cannot be removed or swapped.
Must enhance Chief Gear at least once before upgrading the Charm.
🔱 Hero Gear Ascension & Imbuement
Ascension — Requires Enhancement Lv 100 + Mastery Lv 10. Max enhancement stays 100 after ascension.
Imbuement — Activates after Mythic Gear becomes Legendary. Unlocks at enhancement levels 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100.
Mithril — Material for Imbuement. Worth 8,000 pts in Arsenal Type 2 — ONLY use during the event.
Reforged Mythic Gear can be used as Ascension/Imbuement material.
🐾 Pet System
Unlocks at TC Lv 18 (server must also be open a certain number of days).
Can capture up to 3 Pets.
Each pet can only be tamed and fed 2 times daily.
Pet Items:
• Pet Food — upgrades pets
• Common Taming Mark — refines stats
• Advanced Taming Mark — advanced refinement
Different Pet Skills take effect simultaneously.
Refinement warnings only shown when Total Power drops.
Squad power in Flamedragon Tyrant includes pet stats.
🏝️ Oasis Island (My Island)
Unlocks via dock at TC Lv 19.
Water Essence — Primary currency for upgrades & decorations.
Cacti — Collected by Reservoirs from the island.
Key Buildings:
• Fountain of Life — Generates Water Essence (12h storage cap). At Lv 10 unlocks Golden Sunset Wonder.
• Reservoir — Collects cacti & generates Water Essence. Build multiple.
Each decoration type has a placement limit — extras give no bonus.
Allies can help your Fountain every 6 hours (both earn extra Water Essence).
Worker efficiency is higher when actively viewing the island screen.
📚 Master System
Unlock
TC Lv 25 + server reaches certain age
Journey Supplies
Start with 30 · +20 at UTC 00:00 · +10 each at 08:00 & 16:00 UTC
Lostlands
Unlocks after completing 60 Realm Journeys. Uses Adventure Supplies.
Master Academy — Unlocks after first Master settles in your Town. Used for Badland journeys & Master development.
Master Emblems — Advance Master Relationships. Obtainable via journeys, purchases, passes.
Master Manuscripts — Required to upgrade Master Skills past level 10.
All Masters share one learning queue. Masters have Expertise (passive, auto-activates) and specialty skills.
👥 Alliance System
Ranks: R0 → R5 (R5 = leader)
Leader absent 3 days → R4 can replace
Leader absent 5 days → any member can replace
Territory: Up to 285 Banners. 75% of a resource mine must be inside banner territory to generate resources.
Alliance Vote: Requires Alliance Lv 5 + server open 3+ days. R4+ can initiate. Max 3 simultaneous votes. Results kept 90 days or 20 entries.
Alliance HQ: First build is free; rebuilds cost resources. Once demolished must be fully rebuilt.
🚀 Kingdom Transfer
⚠️ Permanent — cannot return to previous kingdom.
Target kingdom must have same hero generation and Truegold level.
Cost: 1–50 Transfer Passes based on power. Max 85 passes for sale per month.
25-day cooldown between transfers.
Resources exceeding Storehouse Protection limit are lost.
✅ Friends & private chats preserved.
❌ All group chats removed.
Up to 4 characters per kingdom.
🏯 Sanctuary Battle — Key Rules
Eligibility: R4+ rank, alliance must have 20+ members to sign up.
Each alliance can occupy one Sanctuary + one Fortress per phase.
Defense Phase: Shield activates — structures cannot be attacked. NPCs auto-garrison when battles begin.
Time Voting: R4/R5 select 3 preferred time slots. Actual time voted on by all participating alliances.
Outpost buffs remain active during Kingdom of Power events.
🌍 Kingdom of Power (KoP) — Schedule & Rules
Preparation Phase: 5 days + 10 hours
Battle Phase: Starts 10:00 UTC Saturday. 12 hours total:
• First 2 hrs + last 4 hrs: free movement & combat
• Middle 6 hrs: King's Castle attacks only
Matchmaking: Begins Saturday, results published Sunday.
Points: Only earned when level difference with opponent is within 3.
Alliance switches mid-event = accumulated points stay with original alliance.
Troop Revival: 30% system · 10% gems · 50% ally assistance
Auto Rally: Unavailable after crossing servers. Auto Hunting allowed.
Outpost buffs remain effective across kingdoms.
🏟️ Tri-Alliance Clash — Key Rules
Frequency: Monthly. Top 20 alliances by power.
Factions: Earth Guard (upper left) · Storm Guard (upper right) · Tidal Guard (bottom)
⭐ Troops are DUPLICATES — battle results do NOT affect your real soldiers.
Soldiers ARE killed when attacking governors. Healing requires resources.
Energy: Recovers each minute. Building captains regen faster.
Alliance rewards: Based ONLY on Legion 1's ranking.
Shop: Refreshes every 2 weeks.
🐉 Flamedragon Tyrant — Key Rules
Alliance-based battlefield. Up to 60 members per battlefield.
Squad Power = governor gear + charms + hero gear + battle tech + pets
Attacking Palace/Aeries = injured only (not killed).
Attacking governors = soldiers killed (real loss).
Currencies: Dragonclaw Marks · Dragon Essence · Pyrocrystals
Tyrant rewards: 30-day exclusive skin, daily declaration edits, appoints titles/honors.
🏆 Terror Hunt — Mechanics Reference
Start: R4+ alliance members can initiate Terror Hunt.
Alliance change cooldown: Must wait 46 hours between sessions when switching alliances.
Points: Leaving alliance clears ALL ranking points. Rejoining does NOT restore them.
Damage: Rally captain's heroes + RNG determine damage variance. Stronger overall stats don't guarantee higher output.
Traps: Can contribute Hunting Arrows even after trap reaches max level. If trap is removed and rebuilt, all contributors must re-supply arrows from scratch.
