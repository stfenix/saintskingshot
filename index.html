🐾 Pet System
Unlocks at TC Lv 18 (server must also be open a certain number of days).
Can capture up to 3 Pets.
Each pet can only be tamed and fed 2 times daily.
Pet Items:
• Pet Food — upgrades pets
• Common Taming Mark — refines stats
• Advanced Taming Mark — advanced refinement
Different Pet Skills take effect simultaneously.
Refinement warnings only shown when Total Power drops.
Squad power in Flamedragon Tyrant includes pet stats.
🏝️ Oasis Island (My Island)
Unlocks via dock at TC Lv 19.
Water Essence — Primary currency for upgrades & decorations.
Cacti — Collected by Reservoirs from the island.
Key Buildings:
• Fountain of Life — Generates Water Essence (12h storage cap). At Lv 10 unlocks Golden Sunset Wonder.
• Reservoir — Collects cacti & generates Water Essence. Build multiple.
Each decoration type has a placement limit — extras give no bonus.
Allies can help your Fountain every 6 hours (both earn extra Water Essence).
Worker efficiency is higher when actively viewing the island screen.

👥 Alliance System
Ranks: R0 → R5 (R5 = leader)
Leader absent 3 days → R4 can replace
Leader absent 5 days → any member can replace
Territory: Up to 285 Banners. 75% of a resource mine must be inside banner territory to generate resources.
Alliance Vote: Requires Alliance Lv 5 + server open 3+ days. R4+ can initiate. Max 3 simultaneous votes. Results kept 90 days or 20 entries.
Alliance HQ: First build is free; rebuilds cost resources. Once demolished must be fully rebuilt.
🚀 Kingdom Transfer
⚠️ Permanent — cannot return to previous kingdom.
Target kingdom must have same hero generation and Truegold level.
Cost: 1–50 Transfer Passes based on power. Max 85 passes for sale per month.
25-day cooldown between transfers.
Resources exceeding Storehouse Protection limit are lost.
✅ Friends & private chats preserved.
❌ All group chats removed.
Up to 4 characters per kingdom.
🏯 Sanctuary Battle — Key Rules
Eligibility: R4+ rank, alliance must have 20+ members to sign up.
Each alliance can occupy one Sanctuary + one Fortress per phase.
Defense Phase: Shield activates — structures cannot be attacked. NPCs auto-garrison when battles begin.
Time Voting: R4/R5 select 3 preferred time slots. Actual time voted on by all participating alliances.
Outpost buffs remain active during Kingdom of Power events.
🌍 Kingdom of Power (KoP) — Schedule & Rules
Preparation Phase: 5 days + 10 hours
Battle Phase: Starts 10:00 UTC Saturday. 12 hours total:
• First 2 hrs + last 4 hrs: free movement & combat
• Middle 6 hrs: King's Castle attacks only
Matchmaking: Begins Saturday, results published Sunday.
Points: Only earned when level difference with opponent is within 3.
Alliance switches mid-event = accumulated points stay with original alliance.
Troop Revival: 30% system · 10% gems · 50% ally assistance
Auto Rally: Unavailable after crossing servers. Auto Hunting allowed.
Outpost buffs remain effective across kingdoms.
🏟️ Tri-Alliance Clash — Key Rules
Frequency: Monthly. Top 20 alliances by power.
Factions: Earth Guard (upper left) · Storm Guard (upper right) · Tidal Guard (bottom)
⭐ Troops are DUPLICATES — battle results do NOT affect your real soldiers.
Soldiers ARE killed when attacking governors. Healing requires resources.
Energy: Recovers each minute. Building captains regen faster.
Alliance rewards: Based ONLY on Legion 1's ranking.
Shop: Refreshes every 2 weeks.
🐉 Flamedragon Tyrant — Key Rules
Alliance-based battlefield. Up to 60 members per battlefield.
Squad Power = governor gear + charms + hero gear + battle tech + pets
Attacking Palace/Aeries = injured only (not killed).
Attacking governors = soldiers killed (real loss).
Currencies: Dragonclaw Marks · Dragon Essence · Pyrocrystals
Tyrant rewards: 30-day exclusive skin, daily declaration edits, appoints titles/honors.
🏆 Terror Hunt — Mechanics & Level Reference
Start: R4+ alliance members can initiate Terror Hunt.
Alliance change cooldown: Must wait 46 hours between sessions when switching alliances.
Points: Leaving alliance clears ALL ranking points. Rejoining does NOT restore them.
Damage: Rally captain's heroes + RNG determine damage variance. Stronger overall stats don't guarantee higher output.
Traps: Can contribute Hunting Arrows even after trap reaches max level. If trap is removed and rebuilt, all contributors must re-supply arrows from scratch.

🎯 Joiner Hero Mechanics — Key Insights
How Joiners Work
Only the first slot hero of each joiner counts. Their first skill is added as a rally skill (max 4 joiners). Only the first 4 members with a maxed first skill are counted — level your joiner hero's Skill 1 to 5 first.
Mix Chenko + Amane for +12.5% Bonus
4× same hero (e.g. 4 Chenkos) = 2.0× SkillMod (+100%)
2× Chenko + 2× Amane = 2.25× SkillMod (+125%)
Chenko = effect_op 101 · Amane = effect_op 102 · Different identifiers multiply instead of adding. Same applies to Lethality + Attack mixing.
Best Joiner Combos
Attack rally: Chenko + Amane + Yeonwoo + Margot/Vivian
Defense: 4× Saul (better than 4 Gordons) or 3 Saul + 1 Fahd
Bear Hunt: Chenko, Amadeus, Yeonwoo, Amane, Margot — all S-tier joiners
S-tier = can stack 4× for max value. Hilde is A-tier (15% boost vs 25% for S-tier).
🏰 King's Castle Battle — Key Strategy
Double Rally Strategy
Rally 1 (Attack): Amadeus · Petra · Marlin — 50/20/30 formation. Weakens enemy holding the castle.
Rally 2 (Garrison, 5 sec later): Eric · Jaeger · Hilde — 60/20/20 formation. Moves in to take the castle.
If Rally 1 captures first: recall attack heroes so garrison rally secures with defensive setup.
Holding the Castle
Refill troops constantly — watch infantry counts, send garrison rallies to top up.
Joiner combo: Chenko + Amane + Saul + Fahd for best all-round result. Pure offense: 2 Chenko + 2 Amane.
Attack turrets when hold is stable — ignored turrets deal increasing damage.

✓ Use Moose + Grizzly Bear pets
✓ Upgrade Infirmary to handle flood of wounded

🏛️ Tri-Alliance Clash — Battle Strategy
Team Structure
Attack Team — push enemy territory, capture buildings, target Garrisons.
Defender Teams 1 & 2 — protect key routes and your Garrison.
Floaters — conserve energy, react where needed. Critical for Temple phase.
R4s must be present — they assign Captains to buildings (faster energy regen).
Key Objectives by Phase
0–17 min (Seize): Cap all your buildings, secure Transit Hubs and A29/B29/C29 — these are the only paths to Temple.
20 min (Garrisons open): Defend yours (+1800 pts/min). Attack team grabs enemy Garrison.
40 min (Temple): Rush Temple (50k first-cap bonus). Floaters cut enemy transit routes at A29/B29/C29.
Energy & Tips
Energy is everything — use Conscript (♥+ button) to heal between battles only when no fight is happening.
If badly wounded but team holds building: retreat to previous building, heal, return.
Activate pet skills + city buffs before registering — buffs stay for entire event.
Register in same lane to potentially get easier matchmaking.
💎 Tempered Truegold — Weekly Strategy
Unlocks at TG8 (~310 days). Convert TG → Tempered TG via Super Refinement in the Truegold Crucible.
Optimal weekly plan:
• Monday (reset day): Do all 20 Tier 1 refinements immediately (20 TG each → 1–3 TTG, best ratio)
• Tue–Sun: 1 refinement/day only — take the half-cost daily bonus in Tier 2 (25 TG instead of 50)
⚠️ Do NOT rush into Tier 3–5. Cost triples while gain barely increases. Stay Tier 1 on Mondays, Tier 2 rest of week.
First daily refinement of each tier costs half (Tier 1 day 1 = 10 TG, Tier 2 = 25 TG).
⚔️ T10 TG8 vs T11 — Which Path?
For F2P / low / mid spenders the choice is clear:
T10 TG8 is the better path.
T10 TG8 beats T11 TG5 ~95% of the time in combat simulations. T11 only becomes stronger at T11 TG7.

🐻 Terror Hunt (Bear Battle) — Damage Mechanics
Bear is composed of 5,000 special infantry troops with 10 Defense and 83.33 Health each. Battles last 10 turns (unlike normal battles). Your troops don't die, so damage stays constant per round.
Key Mechanics
• Archers deal +10% extra damage vs Bear (Ranged Strike vs infantry)
• Bear Trap level bonus adds +5% Attack per level to all troop types (+25% at Lv 5)
• Since troops don't die, SkillMod stays 1 unless chance-based hero skills are active
• Score = ceil(total 10-round damage)
Damage Formula
attack_per_troop = base_attack × (1 + bonus) × lethality / 100
army = √(your_troops × bear_troops)
bear_defense = Health × Defense / 100 = 8.333
round_dmg = army × attack_per_troop / bear_defense / 100
total_score = ceil(round_dmg × 10)
T6 Example (Bear Lv 5, 6000 each)
army = √(6000 × 5000) = 5,477
Infantry: 5477 × 30.375 / 8.333 / 100 = 199.6
Cavalry: 5477 × 91.25 / 8.333 / 100 = 599.8
Archer: 5477 × 121.75 / 8.333 / 100 × 1.10 = 880.2
Total 10 rounds: 16,797 score
Troop Ratio Tip
Army size uses square root of troop count — stacking only Archers gives diminishing returns. Mix in Cavalry for solid damage. Recommended: ~1% Infantry (minimum), rest split Archer-heavy with meaningful Cavalry.

⚔️ Troop Training vs. Promotion — When to Use Each
General rule: Always train your second-highest tier troops normally, and save promotions for big events (HoG, KvK, Strongest Governor) — promoting during events gives more points than training with the same speedups.
The Core Strategy
• Once you unlock TN, train TN-1 in the background
• During HoG / KvK / Strongest Gov: promote TN-1 → TN for maximum event points
• Between events: keep training camps running on TN-1
• Example: Unlock T9 → train T8 constantly → promote to T9 during events
Infirmary Risk
• Infirmary has limited space — lower-tier troops fill it first
• If your Infirmary fills with T1–T6, your T9/T10 become permanently lost
• Keep low-tier troop counts minimal — promote them off during events
Troop Load & Gathering
• Higher-tier troops carry more resources per march
• More T-max troops = faster farming and looting
• Extra reason to promote lows off and keep your army top-heavy
Event Timing Tip
Start training Tmax-1 troops a few days before HoG so you have a stockpile ready to promote on day 1 of the event. Promoting Tmax-1 consistently outscores both promoting T1s and simply training new troops for the same speedup cost.
🏥 Batch Healing — Save Speedups with Alliance Assists
Batch healing lets you instantly heal troops without spending speedups by splitting healing into small batches that your alliance can finish instantly with assists. Most useful during All Out and Castle Battle when your Infirmary fills up fast.
How It Works
1. Check your Embassy: note Ally Help Time (seconds/assist) and Ally Assists (max assists)
2. Multiply: assists × seconds = total free heal time
3. Send a batch that takes just under that total — alliance assists finish it instantly
4. Repeat with small batches — don't heal everything at once
Embassy Lv 25 Example
Base: 22 assists × 73s = 1,606s (~26 min)
With research (Cooperative Protocols + Treatise):
+15 assists, +150s per assist
37 assists × 223s = 8,251s (~2h 17m)
Tips
• Check how many members are online — you need 37 active members to get all 37 assists
• Start small (e.g. 40-min batch) and increase once you see assist volume
• Research Cooperative Protocols & Treatise in Alliance Tech for a massive multiplier
